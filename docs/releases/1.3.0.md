# ProxyFlow 1.3.0

ProxyFlow 1.3.0 adds Shadowrocket as an evidence-bounded production export
target.

## New

- Shadowrocket `.conf` export is available through New Project, target
  switching, Preview, Export, and Health.
- Real-client acceptance was performed against Shadowrocket 2.2.65 build 2615
  for the documented tested subset.

## Improved

- Target capability and compatibility state is surfaced consistently across
  product workflows.
- Shadowrocket diagnostics fail closed for unsupported or unproven intent
  instead of silently dropping or downgrading semantics.

## Compatibility and safety

- Shadowrocket support is capability-driven and evidence-bounded.
- Mixed IP-CIDR/IP-CIDR6 and GEOIP precedence remains unsupported.
- Materialized subscription export is supported; native remote Shadowrocket
  sources and refresh are not implied.

## Known limitations

- Acceptance evidence is pinned to Shadowrocket 2.2.65 build 2615. This does
  not claim universal compatibility or automatic support for newer builds.
- IPv6 behavior remains unverified.
- DoH/DoT and richer DNS roles remain unsupported.
- Service Rules, arbitrary rule sets, native remote refresh, and unproven
  protocols, transports, and cipher/plugin variants remain unsupported or
  conditional.
- Mihomo remains the default product target.
- sing-box official export remains paused and hidden.
